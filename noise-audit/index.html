Exco Noise Audit

5 mining scenarios · about 15 minutes

Each leader estimates the same five scenarios alone, in tonnes, rands, Mt, trucks and months. Before they start, each one also seals a guess of how much the team will disagree. The audit then shows how far apart identical information pushed your exco, compared with what they expected.
