Your five calls

Know the Score · about 3 minutes

Tell me what you do for a living and I'll give you the five calls that matter most in your kind of work. Seal your gut answer to each one first, before you think it through. Then check your numbers and give your analysis answer. When the outcomes are in, you'll see which was right more often: your gut or your analysis.

This browser isn't keeping data (private browsing or blocked storage). You can still seal your calls: just keep the personal link you'll get afterwards, or your calls will be lost when you close this page.

What do you do for a living?

Your job title and answers are stored by Intuint to calculate scores and improve the questions. Name and email are only kept if you give them and tick the box.

Answer each one in about ten seconds. Don't open a spreadsheet and don't ask anyone. For how sure you are: 50% = unsure, could easily be wrong · 70% = probably right · 90% = I'd bet money on it.

Answer all five to seal. Sealed answers can't be changed.

Sealed.

Your personal link. Open it on any device to see your calls, add your analysis and log outcomes. Keep it private.

Step 2: now check your numbers. Open whatever you'd normally use (reports, the system, last time's figures) and give your analysis answer to each call below. Your gut answers stay locked, so you'll see later which one was right more often.

Then post one of these calls in Call of the week in the Skool group.
