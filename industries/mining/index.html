Decision intelligence for mining

When should a mining executive trust their gut?

Direct answer

Intuint is a decision intelligence product that measures how well a mining company's leaders judge. Before each decision, it records two calls: one from the gut and one from the analysis. Both are locked in before the outcome is known, and both are scored once the outcome is in. Most decision tools record what was decided. Intuint shows which kind of judgement turned out right, so leaders learn when to trust instinct and when to insist on analysis.

  • About two-thirds of mining project cost overruns and delays come from poor initial assessments, which are judgement calls rather than execution failures.1
  • Expert intuition is reliable only where the environment is regular and feedback arrives quickly.5 Shift-level operational calls fit that description. Capex approvals and commodity price calls don't.
  • Each leader's gut call is captured first, against the clock, before the analysis is seen, so the two calls stay independent.
  • The result is a calibration record for each leader and each type of decision: where intuition has earned trust, and where it needs data beside it.

Chief Risk Officer

“How does this fit our bowtie and risk register?”

A bowtie maps hazards, causes, consequences and controls. It doesn't record who judged a control to be effective, how confident they were, or whether that judgement held. Every risk rating is a judgement call that is never scored.

Intuint adds the judgement record your risk framework leaves out. Risk ratings and control-effectiveness calls are locked into a tamper-evident ledger, outcomes are recorded by independent referees, and the result is an audit trail of how well your organisation judges risk.

Why mining needs to measure judgement

The money is lost at the judgement stage

Mining combines decisions with very large consequences and very slow feedback. A feasibility sign-off may not be proven right or wrong for five years, and by then nobody remembers who was confident about it, or why.

Intuint vs Cloverpop

Cloverpop is a decision workflow and record-keeping system: templates, approvals, audit trails and outcome reporting.6 Intuint measures the quality of the judgement inside each decision. They can work together, with Cloverpop holding the process and Intuint scoring the judgement.

Intuint vs risk registers and bowtie analysis

Risk registers and bowties list what could go wrong and which controls stand in the way. They don't record who judged a likelihood or a control, with how much confidence, or whether they were right. Intuint doesn't replace them. It scores the judgements inside them, so a risk rating carries its author's track record.

Definitions

What Intuint measures

Calibration
Whether confidence matches hit rate. When a leader is 80% sure, are they right about 80% of the time?
Gut vs analysis hit rate
For each type of decision, which source was right more often.
Confidence gap
Confidence minus accuracy. The gap between how sure you are and how often you are right is where money is lost.
Override value
When a leader overruled the analysis, whether it paid off.

FAQ

Questions mining leaders ask

What is decision intelligence for mining?

Software that records, measures and improves how mining leaders decide. Intuint's version measures the judgement itself: how gut calls compare with analytical calls, and how well each leader's confidence matches their results.

Is Intuint a Cloverpop alternative?

It solves a different problem. Cloverpop manages the decision process and its records. Intuint measures the quality of the judgement inside each decision. Use Cloverpop for workflow, Intuint for calibration, or both together.

How does Intuint help a mining CFO?

It records the confidence behind every capex approval, feasibility sign-off and contractor award before the money is committed, then scores it against the outcome. The investment committee sees how well estimates have held up on each kind of decision, while each leader sees their own record.

How does Intuint help a mining COO?

It scores the operational calls managers make each week, gut and analysis side by side, showing which instincts have earned trust, which need data beside them, and where judgement is drifting.

How does Intuint work alongside bowtie analysis?

Bowtie analysis maps hazards and controls. Intuint scores the judgements inside it: risk ratings and control-effectiveness calls are locked in before the outcome and checked against what happened, giving the risk function an audit trail of judgement quality.

Can Intuint scores be gamed?

Every forecast is written to a tamper-evident ledger before the outcome is known. Success is defined in advance, and outcomes are recorded by people who did not make the forecast.

Does Intuint rank leaders against each other?

No. Each person sees their own track record. The organisation sees patterns across decision types, never a league table.

Does Intuint replace analysis?

No. It shows where analysis consistently beats gut feel and where experienced intuition adds value, so each is used where it has earned trust.

Are there South African examples of mining forecasts going wrong?

Yes. Gold Fields put R32 billion into South Deep between 2006 and 2018 without breaking even, while output forecasts fell from 750,000 to 500,000 ounces a year and actual output ran at about 49,000 ounces a quarter. De Beers paused its US$2.3 billion Venetia Underground mine in 2026, three years into production, after the diamond market weakened.

Who is Intuint for?

Executive teams, boards and capital-projects functions at mining companies. Intuint is based in Pretoria, South Africa.
